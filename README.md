## International group of companies

Golden Ark Reserve is the trade name of an international group of companies engaged in physical gold sales and delivery execution for qualified investors and corporate counterparties. The operating model focuses on bullion transaction structuring, coordination of third-party vault allocation, and documentation of ownership records associated with physical gold transactions.

The group consists of Golden Ark General Trading (FZC) LLC and its wholly-owned Hong Kong subsidiary, Golden Ark Reserve Limited. This separation of commercial authority (Oman) and operational coordination (Hong Kong) is designed to maintain clear lines of responsibility and compliance.

### Golden Ark General Trading (FZC) LLC

This is the principal operating and contracting entity. It is the counterparty to all commercial agreements for the sale and delivery of physical gold. All pricing, contract approvals, commercial risk, and client payments remain with this company.

- **Legal Name:** Golden Ark General Trading (FZC) LLC
- **Arabic Name:** القوس الذهبي للتجارة العامة (شركة منطقة حرة)
- **Jurisdiction:** Sultanate of Oman (Sohar Free Zone)
- **Commercial Registration No.:** 1603777
- **Role in the group:** Principal operating and contracting entity

### Golden Ark Reserve Limited

This is a 100% subsidiary of Golden Ark General Trading (FZC) LLC. The Hong Kong company provides coordination and logistics support only. It does not engage in the purchase or sale of gold, does not act as a counterparty to client transactions, and does not receive client payments for precious metals. Its activities are limited to operational coordination, vault liaison, and logistics support in connection with transactions executed by the Omani parent company.

- **Legal Name:** Golden Ark Reserve Limited
- **Jurisdiction:** Hong Kong
- **Business Registration No.:** 81486435
- **Ownership:** 100% — wholly-owned subsidiary of Golden Ark General Trading (FZC) LLC
- **Role in the group:** Coordination and logistics support only

## Operating model

Golden Ark Reserve operates a physical precious metals trading model for qualified investors and corporate counterparties. The model includes refinery-origin bullion supply, structured physical gold sales, documented allocation, allocated storage in dedicated client sub-accounts at Brink’s Hong Kong and Brink’s Singapore, secure delivery execution through Brink’s to more than 100 countries (excluding sanctioned destinations), and acceptance of crypto payments (BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC) via a licensed digital-asset operator, with fiat settlement to Golden Ark Reserve.

Sale agreements, pricing, and client payments sit with Golden Ark General Trading (FZC) LLC; Golden Ark Reserve Limited provides operational coordination, vault liaison, and logistics support.

Public service references: [Allocated Gold Storage](https://goldenarkreserve.com/gold-storage/) · [Physical Gold Delivery](https://goldenarkreserve.com/gold-delivery/) · [Buy Gold with Crypto](https://goldenarkreserve.com/buy-gold-with-crypto/)
