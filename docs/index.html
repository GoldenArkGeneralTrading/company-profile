Operating model

Golden Ark Reserve operates a physical precious metals trading model for qualified investors and corporate counterparties. The model includes refinery-origin bullion supply, structured physical gold sales, documented allocation, allocated storage with Brink’s, and secure delivery execution through Brink’s.
